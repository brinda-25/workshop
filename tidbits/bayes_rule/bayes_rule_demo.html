Given the marble you drew, what do you think is the updated probability that the bag is Bag A?

Bayes' Theorem Formula

We calculate the posterior probability P(Bag A | Evidence):

P(A | D) = P(D | A) • P(A) / P(D)
  • • P(A) = Prior probability of Bag A = 0.50
  • • P(D | A) = Likelihood of marble given Bag A = 0.80
  • • P(D | B) = Likelihood of marble given Bag B = 0.30
  • • P(D) = Total probability of drawing this marble = 0.55

Calculation Breakdown:

P(A|D) = (0.80 • 0.50) / 0.55 = 72.73%
